# Marketing Analytics Portfolio Project🎯 
Project OverviewThis project analyzes marketing performance metrics across conversion rates, customer engagement, and customer feedback. The goal of this analysis is to identify key trends, understand content performance, and provide actionable recommendations to optimize future marketing strategies 

# Key Insights 📊. 
## 1. Conversion Rates 
The overall conversion rate varied throughout the year, with a notable dip to 5.0% in October before experiencing a strong rebound to 10.2% in December.January recorded the highest overall conversion rate at 18.5%. This was significantly driven by Ski Boots, which achieved an exceptional 150% conversion rate. May experienced the lowest overall conversion rate at 4.3%, indicating a need to revisit marketing strategies during this period.
## 2. Customer Engagement
Social media views peaked in February and July, but declined from August onwards, showing reduced audience engagement in the latter half of the year.Although absolute clicks and likes were low compared to total views, the click-through rate (CTR) remained strong at 15.37%.Blog content drove the most views—especially in April and July—while social media and video content maintained steady, slightly lower engagement.
## 3. Customer Feedback & Sentiment
Customer ratings remained stable with an average of 3.7 throughout the year, which falls below the target goal of 4.0.The majority of reviews were highly positive, comprising 140 four-star reviews and 135 five-star reviews.Sentiment analysis revealed 275 positive reviews compared to 82 negative reviews.

#  Goals & Actionable Recommendations 🚀
## Goals 
### Increase Conversion Rates: 
Identify factors impacting the conversion rate, highlight where visitors drop off, and suggest improvements to optimize the funnel.
### Enhance Customer Engagement: 
Analyze interaction levels with different content types to inform better strategies and determine what drives the highest engagement.
## Improve Customer Feedback:
Understand recurring themes in customer reviews to guide product and service improvements.

## Actions
### Target High-Performing Products: 
Focus marketing efforts on products with proven high conversion rates, such as Kayaks, Ski Boots, and Baseball Gloves. Implement seasonal promotions during peak months like January and September.
### Revitalize Content Strategy:
Counter declining views by experimenting with interactive videos and user-generated content. Optimize call-to-action placements, particularly during the historically lower-engagement months of September through December.
### Address Mixed and Negative Feedback: 
Implement a feedback loop to analyze and address common issues found in mixed and negative reviews. Follow up with dissatisfied customers to resolve issues and encourage re-rating, aiming to push the average rating closer to the 4.0 target.
